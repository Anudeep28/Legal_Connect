---
name: Product scope
description: The owner's intended relationship between this app, the old legal app, and Astrotalk.
---

Build a separate successor to the owner's `law_consultation` app, not a skin-only redesign or a connection to its live data. Preserve its client, lawyer, administrator, consultation, and legal-document workflows. Astrotalk is the reference for a comparable lawyer-consultation concept, not the subject matter or brand.

**Why:** The owner asked to leverage "all of the framework" from their old app and improve it in every aspect, then explicitly chose "Separate version" rather than sharing existing live data.

**How to apply:** Keep the old live service untouched. Any live-data import or production-service connection requires a new explicit decision. Evaluate additions and redesigns against the full multi-role consultation-to-document product, not just the public marketplace.
