description: Owner-selected refund and legal-content ownership rules.

Consultation payments are refundable only for lawyer cancellation. Unused document purchases are refundable within seven days. Clients retain rights to their legal content.

**Why:** The owner explicitly selected these rules when asked which payment and data rules this app should use.

**How to apply:** Implement these rules for this separate legal app, subject to mandatory applicable law. Do not substitute a client-cancellation refund window or claim platform ownership of client content.
